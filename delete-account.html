Delete Your Facila Account

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains how Facila users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to belhaoua.Asma@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Facila Account Deletion Request.
  3. Send the request from the same email address used to sign in to Facila, so we can verify account ownership.
  4. State clearly that you want your Facila account and associated data deleted.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Your Facila user profile and account association.
  • Your email address stored by Facila.
  • Your saved learning progress.
  • Your premium status stored by Facila, if applicable.

Data Retention

Facila does not intentionally retain account data after the deletion request has been completed, except where retention is required by applicable law. Any data retained for a legal obligation will be kept only for the legally required period.

Important

Deleting your Facila account does not delete your Google Account. It only removes the account and data associated with Facila.

Contact

If you have questions about account deletion, contact belhaoua.Asma@gmail.com.
